Fast pre-screen of trading ideas using a Profit Factor threshold before promoting to a full LEAN backtest. These are not final validated results, see the Backtesting tab for that.

  • TBC: relevant, not yet tested
  • Pass: ≥ 1.2 PF, ready for backtest
  • Parked: promising, unresolved
  • Retired: no edge
  • Anti-edge: worse than random

All backtests use a starting capital of £10,000. Results use OANDA data from Jan 2010 (2004 for VIX-based strategies). A result only passes the 8-point validator if it clears:

  1. t-stat > 3.0
  2. minimum 100 trades
  3. Sharpe > 1.0 after costs
  4. holdout decay ratio ≥ 0.5 (out-of-sample performance vs in-sample)
  5. at least 60% of walk-forward folds profitable
  6. max drawdown < 20%
  7. correlation to other deployed strategies < 0.5
  8. trading costs (swap/financing) subtracted

See the Validator column for each row's overall PASS/FAIL/INCOMPLETE status.
